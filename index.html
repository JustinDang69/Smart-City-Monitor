Smart Air and Noise Quality Monitoring System

Empowering Braybrook with Real-Time Environmental Data for Smarter Decision Making

View Dashboard Learn More

System Capabilities

Air Quality Monitoring

Real-time tracking of PM2.5, NO₂, CO₂, and other pollutants across Braybrook. Helps identify pollution hotspots and trends.

  • Real-time sensor data
  • Pollutant analysis
  • Health impact assessment

Noise Monitoring

Track noise pollution levels from traffic and industry. Identify peak noise periods and high-risk areas for targeted interventions.

  • Continuous monitoring
  • Peak detection
  • Impact mapping

Weather Analysis

Integrate weather data to correlate environmental conditions with pollution levels. Understand seasonal patterns and forecasting.

  • Temperature tracking
  • Wind patterns
  • Precipitation data

Traffic Analysis

Monitor traffic flow and vehicle density to correlate with air and noise pollution. Support smart transportation planning.

  • Vehicle counting
  • Flow analysis
  • Congestion mapping
